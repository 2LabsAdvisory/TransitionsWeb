Resources for families

Understanding disability funding in Alberta can feel like a maze. These plain-language guides help you find your way.

Funding explained

Alberta funding, in plain language

FSCD

Family Support for Children with Disabilities

Funding for families raising a child under 18 with a disability — respite, community support and more. To apply, call (780) 427-4354 and select option 3.

Read it on alberta.ca →
PDD

Persons with Developmental Disabilities

Funding for adults 18+ — community living, employment and everyday life.

Read it on alberta.ca →
AISH

Assured Income for the Severely Handicapped

Monthly financial and health benefits for adult Albertans with a permanent disability.

Read it on alberta.ca →
