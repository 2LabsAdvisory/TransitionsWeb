Ways to give

Your gift builds new tomorrows.

Every dollar you give stays local — funding the programs that help children, youth and adults with developmental disabilities live, work and play in St. Albert and area.

Prefer to give by phone? Call (780) 458-7371 during office hours.

Where your gift goes

Real support, close to home

Early Childhood Intervention

Home visits, playroom sessions and community playgroups for children from birth to 6.

Respite for families

Hours of in-home and out-of-home support that give caregivers a genuine break.

Adult community life

Support to find work, build friendships and take part in life in St. Albert.
