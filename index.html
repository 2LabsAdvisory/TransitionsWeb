Our Purpose

Support for people with developmental disabilities — at every stage of life.

Since 1978, Transitions has helped children, youth and adults in St. Albert, Sturgeon County and northwest Edmonton live, work and play as full members of their community.

Early Childhood

Family Services

Adult Services

People first — always

Building on strengths, not labels.

We provide person- and family-centred support that builds on each person's strengths — so they can lead their own life, form meaningful relationships, and take their place in the community.
